# Worked examples

These are synthetic in-session exercises using the bundled fixtures. They demonstrate the outputs, not customer results or independent model performance.

## A checked quote reply

From the [postcard fixture](../skills/check-agent-job/references/practice.md), the initial draft stated:

> For 120 single-sided 4 × 6 inch postcards on white uncoated stock, the subtotal is $21.60 USD before tax ($0.18 each). We offer local pickup only, so shipping is unavailable.

After the supplied quantity correction, the current draft stated:

> For 180 single-sided 4 × 6 inch postcards on white uncoated stock, the subtotal is $32.40 USD before tax ($0.18 each). We offer local pickup only, so shipping is unavailable.

Both versions said tax, completion timing and pickup hours still needed confirmation. The saved checks mapped claims to the shop notes, recomputed the amounts and confirmed the old quantity/subtotal were absent from the revised draft. The original remained available. `START-HERE.md` linked the current draft, sources, checks and correction for a later return.

## Three response drafts

The [library fixture](../skills/boomscrolling-response-drafts/references/practice.md) supplied a fictional tool-shelf post, an intentionally invalid source URL, one earlier reply and the voice “concise, curious neighbor; warm without hype.” An exact copy of the earlier reply was flagged and discarded.

| Candidate | Draft | Contribution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | For the experiment you describe, a simple tally of tool loans could show which tools people reach for. | Optional measurement |
| B | For that shelf, a labeled return spot might help keep the screwdriver available for the next person. | Practical return detail |
| C | What would make the tool-shelf experiment worth continuing for your library? | Success criterion |

The final three had no lexical flags in six comparisons. A separate in-session review checked voice and distinct meaning; unprovided history remained unknown. Selection initially stayed pending. The fixture's later explicit practice choice saved B with its source, intended voice and `DRAFT — NOT POSTED`. Nothing was posted or connected to an account.
